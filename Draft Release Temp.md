# Draft release notes

> Accumulates until the next tag (WORKFLOW.md § Where things are recorded). Grouped by area; one entry per surface, rewritten to its final state.

## ✨ New Features

- **Allocazione — Consulente** — Added a guided path that sets your allocation with two AI agents: eight screens, one question each («Indietro» always available, the draft kept on your browser) — age and horizon, how much of a loss you can stand shown as euro on your own base, the exchange you buy on, accumulation or distribution, your goals one per screen with «cosa manca?» at the end, whether your home, pension funds and illiquid holdings count in the base, what to keep and avoid, a summary —, then three model calls saved as each lands (the strategy, the instruments found on the web for your exchange, the review) and a report with the proposal against today, the instruments per sub-target, the goals with the instruments behind them, the role changes and «Applica», one checkbox per thing it writes (targets and sub-targets, asset roles, goals and quotas), with a date to review the plan by printed in the Allocazione header once applied. Under the report, «Chiedi una modifica» rewrites report and proposal on a request in your words in a couple of minutes (the review alone is run again; the requests stay listed on the report). Each of the three model calls is its own request, so a run fits the platform's five-minute limit and a stage that fails is retried from itself. Redoing the path later starts from the plan in force and its reasons — the last report, the changes you asked for, when you applied it — and the new report says what it confirms and what it changes. In the thirty days before the review date, and after it, the «Consulente» button of Allocazione becomes «Rivedi con il Consulente» and the report says how close the date is («La revisione del piano è vicina: entro il 31/10/2027, fra 12 giorni»). Reached from the «Consulente» button of Allocazione and from a row under the Assistente's conversation; not on the demo account.

- **Cashflow › Budget — «Fuori dal budget»** — Added a checkbox on a spending row for a purchase made for someone else and refunded later: the row stays in your cashflow (Tracciamento, Analisi, Panoramica, Storico), but the month's ceiling, the category budgets, their projections and alerts and the budget emails no longer count it; a series or an instalment plan takes the flag on every occurrence, the feed and the table show a «fuori budget» chip and the row's detail a «Budget» line.
- **Patrimonio · Panoramica — Intestatari** — Added the «Intestatari» of an asset, by quota: in the asset form one row per household member of Impostazioni › Famiglia, each in percent of the asset or in quote (euro for an account, a property or a loan) — a % | quote switch, percent by default where the value is money, so a joint account stays 50/50 as its balance moves —, the other figure derived beside it, and the last row the rest, computed — «In comune» by default (nothing to migrate: an asset with no quota is shared), or the last member with «Nessuna quota in comune», who then also takes the quote a trade adds later; a sum past the quantity is refused in the form, never saved. A pension fund belongs whole to its Previdenza member, a mortgage follows its property's quotas. When at least one asset names a person, an «Intestatari» tile on Panoramica and Patrimonio says who holds what share of the net worth («Il 54% è intestato a Marco, il 31% a Giulia, il resto in comune.»), one row per person with amount, share and how many assets they hold a quota of, the debts counted against their owner — a credit card in the red included; an owner deleted from the settings reads «Senza intestatario». In Patrimonio › Strumenti an «Intestatario» filter narrows the list to a person's quotas and the row's sub-line names them («di Marco» for a whole asset, «Marco 50% · Giulia 50%» or «Marco 100 · Giulia 20 · 30 in comune» for a divided one). A sale that leaves fewer quote than the ones assigned is declared — «quote da riassegnare» on the row and in the tile's footer — never rescaled in silence. The tile has a «Valore | Carico» switch in its corner: «Carico» splits the same rows by what each person put in — quantity × the purchase price of every quoted instrument, an account, a property, a loan or a pension fund at its value — so a household that funded its investments evenly sees it even when today's gains sit on one side («Al prezzo di carico, il 55% è intestato a Marco, il 31% a Giulia, il resto in comune.»); the choice is remembered on that browser for Panoramica and Patrimonio alike, and an instrument without a purchase price in euro counts at its value and is named in the footer.
