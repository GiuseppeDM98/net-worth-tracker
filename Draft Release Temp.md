## ✨ New Features

- **Storico reads any month under the mouse.** Moving the pointer along the Evoluzione line puts the whole page in that month: the value at the top of the tile glides to that month's figure with its change on the month before, Composizione lists that month's split (the rows re-rank as you move), Valore per strumento jumps to it when the snapshot has the per-instrument breakdown, and the Driver bars light that month. Leave the line or press Escape to come back to today. The words never change — only the figures move — and nothing is saved: the month you picked in Valore per strumento is still there when you let go. On a phone or a tablet the line stays a shape and its tooltip keeps the reading.
- **Rendimenti changes period without redrawing.** Switching YTD → 1 anno → Storico no longer blanks and rebuilds the tiles: the TWR glides from the old figure to the new, «Crescita di 100» and «Capitale e mercato» bend into the new window, the heatmap fades cell by cell and its years appear or leave, and «Da dove viene il rendimento» re-ranks its rows and slides its bars. Only «Aggiorna», which re-reads the data, still dims the page while it waits. With reduced motion on, everything jumps as before.
- **Moving between pages is one scene.** A click in the sidebar, the bottom pill or the «Altro» drawer transitions the content region as a whole: the header line and the verdict of the page you leave become those of the page you open, in place, while the shell stays still. Browsers without the View Transitions API (Firefox) keep the previous fade; reduced motion skips the scene. The theme toggle's circle reveal is unchanged.

- Added **BTP€i support** (#341). «Dettagli Cedole» now asks which inflation mechanism a bond follows: none, BTP Italia (the period's FOI inflation is added to the fixed rate and paid with every coupon) or BTP€i (the real rate is multiplied by the Eurostat HICP indexation coefficient, and the revaluation accrues in that coefficient until maturity). For a BTP€i the form takes today's coefficient, every price refresh multiplies the Borsa Italiana quote (a real price) by the latest coefficient you entered, a trade asks for the coefficient at its own date and remembers it, and the coupon is generated as real rate × coefficient — provisional at the latest known coefficient until you enter the payment-date one from the Dividendi tab ("Cedola semestrale — tasso reale 0,2% × coefficiente di indicizzazione 1,27 = 0,254% del nominale"). Borsa Italiana does not publish the coefficient: copy it from the MEF table or your contract note; without it the position is valued at par of the real price, and the form says so. Redemption at maturity is still not an event for any bond.
- Added **zero-coupon bonds**: a bond saved with a coupon rate of 0 (BOT, CTZ, a zero-coupon BTP) keeps its details — maturity, nominal, indexation — and generates no 0 € coupon.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Fixed **a bond without a «Valore Nominale» being worth 100× too much** (#340). A Borsa Italiana quote is a percentage of par, always: with the nominal empty one unit is now 1 € of nominal — the quantity is the nominal in euro, as on a broker statement — so 1.000 € of a BTP quoted at 93 is worth 930 €, not 93.000 €. Coupons, the final premium and the daily price update already assumed that unit; only the price did not. A nominal of 1000 keeps meaning 1.000 € lots. Positions saved wrong before the fix heal their current price at the next update; their purchase price is corrected from the Registro (the form shows the stored figure back as a quote).
- Fixed **editing a bond tripping the browser's own validation**: the manual price and the purchase price come back as Borsa Italiana quotes with five decimals (96,74843), and a fixed input step refused the form's own value on every edit of a BTP.

- **A falling month is no longer blamed on the market alone.** The Panoramica, Patrimonio and the monthly email used to say "il mercato ha pesato" whenever prices fell, however small that fall was next to what you did yourself. On the real account September 2026 fell by 4.938 € with the market at −1.079 €: the rest was the capital-gains tax the broker withheld on an ETF sale, which in regime amministrato leaves the account the day you sell, with no cashflow row to explain it. The verdict now reads the month's sales from the trade ledger — what was sold, the realized gain, and the tax estimated from each instrument's rate — and names the cause in the order of its weight: "Settembre è in calo: il mercato ha pesato, le tasse sulle vendite di più." Under it, the change is split into what the market did and what you did ("Di quel movimento, −1079 € viene dal mercato e −3859 € dai tuoi movimenti."), then the sale is told in the past tense, because the tax is already gone: "Hai venduto VWCE per 39.052 € con una plusvalenza di 15.726 € e pagato circa 4089 € di tasse." — "circa", because it is an estimate (on the real account it landed 3,64 € under the broker's figure). A month whose own outflows outweigh the market without a taxed sale reads "più per le uscite che per il mercato"; a loss carries no tax; an instrument without a tax rate says the tax is not estimated instead of printing zero.
- **The monthly email's "mercato" figure no longer swallows the tax.** The email measures the market as what is left of the month's change after your savings; a tax paid with no cashflow row therefore counted as a market loss. It is now its own part of the split — "−1837 € viene dal mercato, +988 € da quanto hai risparmiato e −4089 € dalle tasse sulle vendite" — the three still adding up to the change, and the Patrimonio tile's footer names it "circa".
- **Cashflow › Tracciamento can filter the movements by person.** With Divisione on, the Movimenti list gains an "Intestatario" filter — "Tutti · In comune · Giuseppe · Marcella", plus "Senza intestatario" only when the period holds rows whose owner was removed — on the desktop toolbar and inside the phone's "Filtri" drawer; it counts on the badge and resets with "Ripristina" like every other list filter. The tile's reading follows the rows it lists, so picking a person answers "quanto ha speso Giuseppe questo mese" in one line. Every row attributed to a person now also prints that person's name as a chip, in the feed, the table and the detail sheet; shared rows stay as they were. With the feature off nothing changes.
