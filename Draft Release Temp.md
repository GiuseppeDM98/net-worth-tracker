## ✨ New Features

- **A falling month is no longer blamed on the market alone.** The Panoramica, Patrimonio and the monthly email used to say "il mercato ha pesato" whenever prices fell, however small that fall was next to what you did yourself. On the real account September 2026 fell by 4.938 € with the market at −1.079 €: the rest was the capital-gains tax the broker withheld on an ETF sale, which in regime amministrato leaves the account the day you sell, with no cashflow row to explain it. The verdict now reads the month's sales from the trade ledger — what was sold, the realized gain, and the tax estimated from each instrument's rate — and names the cause in the order of its weight: "Settembre è in calo: il mercato ha pesato, le tasse sulle vendite di più." Under it, the change is split into what the market did and what you did ("Di quel movimento, −1079 € viene dal mercato e −3859 € dai tuoi movimenti."), then the sale is told in the past tense, because the tax is already gone: "Hai venduto VWCE per 39.052 € con una plusvalenza di 15.726 € e pagato circa 4089 € di tasse." — "circa", because it is an estimate (on the real account it landed 3,64 € under the broker's figure). A month whose own outflows outweigh the market without a taxed sale reads "più per le uscite che per il mercato"; a loss carries no tax; an instrument without a tax rate says the tax is not estimated instead of printing zero.
- **The monthly email's "mercato" figure no longer swallows the tax.** The email measures the market as what is left of the month's change after your savings; a tax paid with no cashflow row therefore counted as a market loss. It is now its own part of the split — "−1837 € viene dal mercato, +988 € da quanto hai risparmiato e −4089 € dalle tasse sulle vendite" — the three still adding up to the change, and the Patrimonio tile's footer names it "circa".
- **Cashflow › Tracciamento can filter the movements by person.** With Divisione on, the Movimenti list gains an "Intestatario" filter — "Tutti · In comune · Giuseppe · Marcella", plus "Senza intestatario" only when the period holds rows whose owner was removed — on the desktop toolbar and inside the phone's "Filtri" drawer; it counts on the badge and resets with "Ripristina" like every other list filter. The tile's reading follows the rows it lists, so picking a person answers "quanto ha speso Giuseppe questo mese" in one line. Every row attributed to a person now also prints that person's name as a chip, in the feed, the table and the detail sheet; shared rows stay as they were. With the feature off nothing changes.
