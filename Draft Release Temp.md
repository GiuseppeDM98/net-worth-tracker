## ✨ New Features

- An investment's **Sottocategoria** is now genuinely optional. The label reads "Sottocategoria (opzionale)" instead of carrying an asterisk, the picker opens on a "Nessuna" entry above your own sleeves, and choosing it on an asset that already had one clears the field for good — through both of the app's save paths, so a price refresh or a replay of the trade ledger can never put it back. Until now the field was declared optional and enforced as mandatory: on any asset class where you have sub-categories enabled, saving was refused with "La sottocategoria è obbligatoria per questa classe di asset", so an instrument you did not want to slice could not be saved at all.
- Allocazione now states the euros that carry no sleeve instead of hiding them. They appear at the foot of their asset class as a "Senza sottocategoria" row with its share, its value and the words "senza target" — no COMPRA / VENDI chip, no gap, no target tick, because the answer there is "classify it", not "buy" or "sell". Those euros used to count in the class total and in none of its sub-categories, so every sleeve you had set a target for read under target by exactly the unclassified share, and the money itself appeared nowhere on the page. Both plans read the row as an absence of opinion rather than as a 0% target: "Versa" never proposes to buy into it, and "Preleva" draws from it pro-rata instead of emptying it first.

- Redesigned the sign-in and sign-up pages in the same shape as the rest of the app — the Overview, Patrimonio, the four Cashflow tabs, Analisi, Rendimenti, Storico, the Hall of Fame, Allocazione, the five FIRE tabs, Previdenza, the Assistant and Impostazioni — scaled down to what these two pages actually are: one column, one card. Above the card sits the sentence the app is about — "Il tuo patrimonio, spiegato prima che misurato." — and under it what the account holds. On the sign-up page the sentence changes with what is actually possible: "Bastano un'email e una password." when anyone can register, with "La registrazione è riservata alle email autorizzate: se la tua non lo è, te lo diciamo prima di creare qualsiasi cosa." added when the invite list is on, and "Le registrazioni sono chiuse." — with the form replaced by a link to the sign-in page — when they are closed.
- The card now tells you where you are in one line, where the eyebrow's answer used to be: "Accedi con l'email e la password del tuo profilo." while you type, "Sto verificando le credenziali." while it works, "Accesso riuscito: apro la Panoramica." when it lands, and the reason in red when it does not. The status paragraph that used to sit under the button is gone — it was the same information in a second place.
- The password requirements are two rows that tick as you type — "Almeno 6 caratteri" and "Le due password coincidono" — instead of a rule you discover on submit. They are exactly the two rules the form enforces, nothing more.
- The fields themselves follow the app's own form style at last: a 12px label above a 36px input with the standard focus ring, rather than each input boxed inside its own bordered panel. Showing a password is a "Mostra / Nascondi" word instead of an eye icon, and every control on a phone is a 44px target. "Prova la demo" and "Accedi con Google" are unchanged and sit inside the card, under a divider.
