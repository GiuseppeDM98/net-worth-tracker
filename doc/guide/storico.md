## History and Snapshot Baselines

- Annual deltas use December of the previous year as baseline; Patrimonio `Anno Corrente` uses the previous month as a
  **hidden** baseline.
- **A snapshot is a frozen photo**: adding an asset never updates an existing one, so a Storico chart "missing" an asset
  you just added is a stale current-month snapshot, not a bug. **The snapshot cron runs DAILY — the name lies**
  (`0 18 * * *`, no day-of-month guard): storage granularity is monthly, write frequency daily.
- **Reuse `byAsset.totalValue` for historical per-instrument value — never recompute** (it already went through
  `calculateAssetValue()`); aggregate in `snapshotAssetBreakdown.ts`. **Gotcha**: `byAsset` is a newer field, so a month
  picker built on it must filter to non-empty `byAsset` — the resulting gaps are correct.
- **`byAsset.price` is RAW NATIVE CURRENCY**, so `totalValue ≠ quantity × price` for USD/GBp/real-estate; the per-unit EUR
  figure is `u = totalValue / quantity`, and attribution is `priceEffect = q_prev·(u_curr−u_prev)` + `quantityEffect =
  (q_curr−q_prev)·u_curr` (sum = Δ exactly). **A row at quantity 0 is a CLOSED position** (2026-09-06): the cron writes every asset, sold ones included, and read as present its unit value is 0 — a 14.830 € sale printed as «prezzo −14.830 €» on the real account (Xtrackers Overnight, agosto 2026). `attributeSelectedChange` now treats `quantity ≤ 0` as absent on both sides, so the sale is all quantity and the rebuy from that row a pure open; the row itself is still listed at 0 €.
- **A snapshot document has two natures, and only one of them is recomputed.** The figures
  (`totalNetWorth`, `byAssetClass`, `byAsset`, `assetAllocation`, `pension`…) are derived from the assets on every
  write; the **note** the user types on Storico is derived from nothing. Both writers replace the document with a bare
  `.set()` — correct for the figures, since a Firestore `merge` keeps the `byAssetClass` key of an asset class that has
  LEFT the portfolio, a snapshot that can never go back down — so the note has to ride across the replace explicitly:
  `preserveUserAuthoredSnapshotFields` (`lib/utils/snapshotUserFields.ts`), called by
  `app/api/portfolio/snapshot/route.ts` and `.../snapshot/manual/route.ts`. Until 2026-09-07 neither did, and since the
  cron runs DAILY on the CURRENT month a note typed there was erased the same evening — a note on a PAST month
  survived, which is why the bug read as «dopo qualche giorno». **A new `MonthlySnapshot` field that no pipeline
  derives goes in `SNAPSHOT_USER_AUTHORED_FIELDS` or the cron eats it too.** Both snapshot writers REPLACE the
  document, so a new `MonthlySnapshot` field the pipeline does not recompute goes in `SNAPSHOT_USER_AUTHORED_FIELDS`
  or the daily cron erases it (AGENTS.md § *Firestore Writes*).
- **TWR neutralises a cash flow only when the net-worth drop and the flow land in the SAME monthly snapshot** — the fix
  is data entry, never re-bucketing cash flows or excluding cash (doc/guide/rendimenti.md § Per-page blind spots has the mirror case).
- **Two CAGR formulas, intentionally different**: Storico's verdict = `(endNW/startNW)^(12/months) − 1` (wealth growth, said «versamenti inclusi»),
  Rendimenti = `(endNW/(startNW+netCashFlow))^(1/years) − 1` (investment return).
- **A form that CROSS-VALIDATES a sum against a declared total must offer a field for EVERY member of the union.**
  `CreateManualSnapshotModal` refuses to write unless `sumClassAmounts(byClass)` matches the declared `totalNetWorth`
  within 0.01, so a class it does not ask for does not make the form incomplete — it makes an honest snapshot
  **impossible**: with `trendFollowing` or `carry` in the portfolio the six hard-coded fields could never reach the
  total, and every attempt was refused with an arithmetic message that named no cause. The fields are generated from
  `ASSET_CLASS_SEQUENCE` with `ASSET_CLASS_LABELS`, and the pure helpers live in `lib/utils/manualSnapshotAmounts.ts`
  (`emptyClassAmounts` · `parseAmount` · `sumClassAmounts`). **The sum iterates the SEQUENCE, never the record's own
  keys**, so a stale key left by an older document cannot inflate the figure the user is asked to reconcile. The values
  stay STRINGS — an `<input type="number">` where an empty field, a half-typed `1.` and a `0` are three different
  things — and parsing happens once, at the boundary. The same test that pins one field per union member is what a
  future widening trips on.

## Storico — a verdict over tiles (`app/dashboard/history/page.tsx`, `components/history/tiles/*`, `lib/utils/{storicoSummary,storicoNarrative}.ts`)

- **The page reads SIX keys and composes ONE read state** (2026-09-29): `useSnapshots`, `useAssets`, `useSettings`
  (its `targets` are what `getTargets` used to re-read — the same document twice), `useExpenses`, `useAssetTransactions`
  and `usePensionContributions`, shared with the other pages, so after Patrimonio or Analisi the page opens on the
  cache — the expenses WHOLE, by declared need: the Driver splits every year recorded, so this page has no window to
  read, unlike Cashflow's tabs and FIRE since 2026-09-30 (doc/guide/cashflow.md § Expenses by window; it shares that
  list with Analisi and Centri di Costo, not with Tracciamento); `composeReadState` (`lib/utils/readState.ts`) turns the six into `loading` / `loadFailed` — one failed read is
  the page's alert (pinned, seen red). Until then a `Promise.all` of seven direct reads ran on every visit. «Riprova»
  and a new manual snapshot invalidate the keys; a saved note is patched into the snapshots cache (`setQueryData`).
- **The page has NO axis, and its growth is WEALTH growth.** `summarizeGrowth` measures first → latest snapshot with contributions included, and every sentence that prints its CAGR says «versamenti inclusi»; never feed it to a surface that means an investment return (that is Rendimenti's `(endNW/(startNW+netCashFlow))^(1/years)`, § History and Snapshot Baselines).
- **ONE pace for the whole page** (`summarizeGrowthPace`): the trailing-12-month average monthly increase in EURO, linear. It decides the headline (`accelerating` above the lifetime monthly average ×1.10, `slowing` below ×0.90, `steady` between, `losing` when the year is negative) AND `projectNextDoubling`. Both need the snapshot of EXACTLY twelve months earlier (a gap → `trailingDelta: null`, no clause, no projection) and the verdict needs `PACE_MIN_HISTORY_MONTHS` (24) of history; a projection beyond `PROJECTION_MAX_MONTHS` (600) is `null`, never a date. Do not "improve" it with a compound extrapolation: contributions do not compound.
- **A month is a pair of snapshots exactly one calendar month apart** (`summarizeMonthlyMoves`, `withMonthDeltas`): a gap is not a month, a zero delta is neither rising nor falling. The verdict names the best month, the Evoluzione tile the worst — never both in one place.
- **The verdict's «ultimo raddoppio» is always the GEOMETRIC one**; the Raddoppi tile follows its toggle (`prepareDoublingTimeData(ordered, mode)` — feed it SORTED snapshots, oldest first, it does not sort). `describeDoublings` reads `progressPercentage` from the milestone in progress and says «il primo» (no noun) when nothing is completed yet.
- **Driver is floored at `cashflowHistoryStartYear`** (`selectDriverYears`, the monthly rows filtered the same way): before it there are no transactions and «mercato» would silently be the whole growth. A year's window runs from the month AFTER its baseline snapshot to the month of its last one, summed over every consecutive pair of snapshots in it (`buildYearlyGrowthDrivers`), so a year and its months add up by construction; the reading names the window («Da gennaio ad agosto 2026», `describeRunningWindow`).
- **The Driver is SIX parts, not two** (2026-09-19, `lib/utils/growthDrivers.ts`): `Δ = risparmio + mercato − tasse + mutuo + fondo pensione + altre`, and **«altre» is the remainder named by cause since 2026-11-12** — `altre = fondo pensione non tracciato + quantità fuori dal registro + saldi dei conti` (`otherPension`, `otherHoldings`, `otherAccounts`; the third is `other − otherPension − otherHoldings`, so the identity cannot break and an unmeasured pair has all three at 0). Until then «mercato» was `Δ − (entrate − uscite)` and read −557 € in settembre 2026, a month the portfolio GAINED ~2.086 €: the broker's withheld tax on a VWCE sale (4.089 € estimated, 4.092,50 € on the statement — regime amministrato, no cashflow row) and 1.297 € of instalments still in calendar all landed in «mercato». Now: **risparmio** counts only the rows already happened (Italian day ≤ today, like the snapshot that closes the month); **mercato** is MEASURED per instrument whenever both snapshots carry `byAsset` — the held quantity's price move, the month's trades from trade price to the snapshot (`tradeAwarePriceEffect` in `marketEffect.ts`, the Panoramica's rule, so the two pages print the same September market), a pension fund net of its contributions from `pensionReturnStartMonth` on, real estate GROSS of debt; **tasse** is `summarizePeriodSales`' estimate; **mutuo** is the fall of the real-estate debt between two snapshots (gross − net, both from `byAsset`: the cashflow books the whole instalment, the principal stays in the net worth — its own name so «risparmio» stays Cashflow's net, owner's call); **fondo pensione** is what Previdenza records as paid in (TFR, employer, voluntary), from the same start month; **altre** is the remainder, in three parts. **«Fondo pensione non tracciato»** (`otherPension`): the funds' value change that is neither measured market nor a recorded contribution — all their growth before `startMonth`. **«Quantità fuori dal registro»** (`otherHoldings`): for every instrument that exists today and is not an account, a fund, a loan or a property, its value change that neither its price nor its recorded buys and sells explain (`splitOther` reads the SAME per-instrument market `measuredMarket` summed, and `tradeMoneyIn` counts fees exactly as `tradeAwarePriceEffect` does) — units added or corrected by hand, an adjustment, the ledger's baseline, a hand-valued stake such as Private Equity revalued (it lives in `quantity` at price 1). **«Saldi dei conti»** (`otherAccounts`): the rest — balances typed on another day than the rows (a credit card is debited the month AFTER its rows, so it swings and gives back), a debt corrected by hand, the gap between estimated and withheld tax (the app records no withheld total per month: declared here, not measurable apart), assets deleted since. A pair without `byAsset` falls back to `Δ − risparmio + tasse − fondo pensione` as market with mutuo and altre at 0, and the footer names the first measured month («da dicembre 2025»). The shares (`resolveDriverShares`) are of the two ENGINES only (risparmio vs mercato, the market's the remainder so they sum to 100), never of the growth — since 2026-09-20 they are no longer printed in the sentence (a bare «(20%)» had no referent once four more parts followed): they are the split bar's widths and its accessible name. How the parts are SHOWN is the next bullet. The split bar shows only the POSITIVE engines; a negative one reads in words. The monthly bars are three side by side (risparmio, mercato, tasse in `--chart-4`), never stacked; mutuo, fondo pensione and the three parts of altre live in the hover (`driverRestParts`, the ledger's words shortened: «fondo non tracciato», «fuori registro», «saldi dei conti») and in the year's ledger (owner: «riga + tooltip, niente barra»); the Dettaglio's month tooltip and the sr-only table print the same three parts (the same month never reads three parts in one tile and one figure in the next).
- **The six parts are a LEDGER, not a sentence** (2026-09-20, critique `.impeccable/critique/2026-09-20T09-25-18Z__app-dashboard-history-page-tsx.md` (deleted on 2026-10-17 with every critique: in `git log`), its figures redacted on 2026-10-15 — nothing that identifies the owner in the repo, WORKFLOW.md § Where things are recorded). The reading is two clauses — the growth, then the two engines with the heavier FIRST and no share («21.916 € dal mercato e 5364 € dal risparmio») — closed by the rest as ONE netted flow («Il resto, voce per voce qui sotto, vale +4638 €»; a single part is still named, «si compensa» when it nets to zero: `describeDriverRest`; when exactly ONE of the three parts of «altre» is printed non-zero the clause names it by its ledger label — «Il resto: +400 € di saldi dei conti» — and says «di altre variazioni» only when two or three are, owner, 2026-11-12). The bars' legend is one entry per SERIES («Mercato (rosso se in perdita)» with two swatches: the red is a state of a bar, not a fourth series) and sits under «Ultimi 12 mesi»; their figures live in a `sr-only` table after the SVG, not in one `aria-label` of 72 figures.
- **«Mutuo» follows the linked instalments by itself** (2026-09-25): a `debt` row linked to a loan lowers its
  principal by the principal on its date, before the snapshot of that evening (doc/guide/cashflow.md), so the
  Driver's «mutuo» measures the plan without a monthly edit; a debt typed by hand still lands there too.
- **«Mutuo» reads the LOAN rows since 2026-10-10** (doc/guide/patrimonio.md: a debt is a `loan` asset worth minus its
  principal, `byAsset.totalValue < 0`): `debtRepaidBetween` sums `debtRepaymentsBetween` (`lib/utils/marketEffect.ts`
  since 2026-10-14, the per-debt rule the Panoramica and the email name by asset — a property's legacy debt is
  credited to the loan that finances it, the total unchanged) over the loans as a TOTAL (previous − current) — a loan
  opened in the month is debt that went up (negative «mutuo»), one deleted after its payoff is repaid — and keeps the
  legacy property pairing (gross − net, per property on both sides) for the snapshots written before the migration and
  for a property the migration never reaches; the two parts cross the migration month without a jump (the legacy
  debt falls to zero, the loan opens by the same figure, pinned by `__tests__/growthDrivers.test.ts`). A loan row
  is skipped by `measuredMarket` (a debt has no price: its unit value is −1 by construction, so its price effect would
  be 0 anyway) and is told from a legacy row by `isLoanRow` — the asset's type, or a negative `totalValue` with a
  positive quantity for a loan deleted since. The REIT rule holds: `realEstateIds` is by TYPE, so a loan of class
  realestate is never read gross.

Until then the recap filtered `>= startYear` with NO right edge (208 rows through 2043 on the real account: «Risparmiato da lavoro» 7.215 € where the closed window says 15.447 €, the market overstated by the same 8.231 €) and counted the baseline's own month too. ## Composizione mobile

Composed for a small screen on 2026-10-14 (its spec, retired on 2026-10-15, is in `git log -- doc/mobile/`; the primitives and the sample page are Hall of Fame's, doc/guide/hall-of-fame.md § Composizione mobile): below `desktop:` the page is composed, at 1440 it is the page above with one declared change — the «ultimi 12 mesi» chip prints a figure that rounds to zero unsigned and uncoloured, «0 € (0,0%)» (the printed-zero rule, AGENTS.md § Layout and Color Tokens: the euro since 2026-10-14, the percentage since 2026-10-15; until then «+0 € (+0,0%)» in green). The rules are DESIGN.md's (§ 5 → Small-Screen Composition: The First-Screen, Lifted-Figure, Closed-Row and Binding-Clause Rules); here is what this page does with them.

- **THE tile**: Evoluzione (`storico-evoluzione`), open at every width; its curve yields to 120px below `desktop:` (the chart yields, never the reading: The First-Screen Rule).
- **The strip** (`selectStoricoStrip({ pace, featured, ledger, window })`, `storicoSummary.ts`, importing only types from `storicoNarrative.ts`): «Ultimi 12 mesi» (`pace.trailingDelta`) → «Il perché» — with less than a year of history the cell says «meno di un anno di storico» and opens THE tile, since there is no «Il perché» to open (seen on the base fixture); **ONE cell of the Driver**, «Risparmio {anno}» — «Risparmio 2026 · gen–set» on a running year (`describeRunningWindowShort`, the Driver row's window) — the featured year's savings exactly as its ledger row prints them (`formatDriverEuro`, the formatter `DriverTile` uses; signed, uncoloured: a flow; «0 €»), → `storico-driver` (2026-10-10: one Driver cell, the market stays in the open ledger). `featured === null` → no Driver cell; fewer than two snapshots → no strip.
- **Lifted blocks** (`EvoluzioneTile` `liftedFigures`, guard `isEvoluzioneLiftedBlock`): the first two chips WHOLE — the first sentence restates the growth, its percentage and the CAGR — and the euro of the «ultimi 12 mesi» chip, the strip's cell, whose percentage stays alone (The Lifted-Figure Rule); the row of chips hides whole when nothing in it is left. No row is `failed`: the six reads are one state (`composeReadState`).
- **`binding` clauses and the `scope`**: «versamenti inclusi» is `binding`: it is in the lead anyway, and the flag keeps it there if the cut ever moves. No `scope`: the page passes none to `PageVerdict`.

- **Absences**: the whole page's, never a row's. ## Per-page blind spots

- **Storico**: `e2e/history.spec.ts` and `e2e/history.mobile.spec.ts` assert STRUCTURE only (the base fixture's «saldi dei conti» is huge — two snapshots, a month of expenses — so no amount is pinned); the pace and the next-doubling projection need the snapshot of EXACTLY twelve months earlier and the pace verdict 24 months of history; the projection is linear, dropped beyond fifty years; the Driver shows only years from `cashflowHistoryStartYear` (untracked income lands in «mercato» where `byAsset` is missing, in «saldi dei conti» where it is not) and its bars the last twelve CALENDAR months; its market is measured only from the first pair of snapshots that both carry `byAsset` (dicembre 2025 on the real account) and before `pensionReturnStartMonth` a pension fund's whole growth is «fondo pensione non tracciato»; «saldi dei conti» swings month to month with a credit card (the rows in one month, the debit in the next) and nets out over a year, and in a ledger it also carries the euros of rounding (it is the remainder BY CONSTRUCTION, so it can differ by a few € from `GrowthDrivers.otherAccounts`); a property and a deleted asset are never in «quantità fuori dal registro» (a property moves by transfers and by its debt, a deleted asset has no type to read: both stay in «saldi dei conti»), a hand-valued stake's revaluation is; the tax is an ESTIMATE (the rate × the realized gain, no minusvalenze pregresse); a property present on one side only moves no «mutuo»; the Lavoro recap follows the Driver's windows, so a year the Driver skips (snapshots but no cashflow row) is absent from its Select and from the cumulative too, and its figures never match a Cashflow total that includes the rows in calendar — compare it with the Driver «Dal {startYear}»; the Lavoro net can be negative on a positive gross (taxes on ALL latent gains, cumulative recap only); the Evoluzione Y axis prints full amounts under 10.000 € of span; the Dettaglio's open state and the chosen month are session state, not in the URL (a month cannot be linked); at 1440 the chips repeat three figures of the verdict by design (the verdict says, the hero shows — as on the Panoramica; below `desktop:` they are lifted, the tablet included since 2026-10-16, § Composizione mobile); `--chart-1` in dark is the hero line at ~2,6:1 (doc/guide/temi.md); the light remainder of a 3px track (`bg-muted` on a card) is nearly invisible, app-wide. The note under «Risparmio e mercato per mese» (`DRIVER_MONTHLY_NO_BAR_NOTE`, `storicoNarrative.ts`) names the parts that have no bar by the SAME labels the month tooltip prints (2026-11-14, pinned by `storicoNarrative.test.ts`): a part renamed there is renamed in the note, and «altre variazioni» stays only in «Lavoro e investimenti».
