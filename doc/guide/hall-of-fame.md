## Hall of Fame — a verdict over tiles (`app/dashboard/hall-of-fame/page.tsx`, `components/hall-of-fame/*`, `lib/utils/{hallOfFameSummary,hallOfFameNarrative}.ts`)

- **The page has NO axis, and it re-derives nothing.** A record is a POSITION, not a period, so there is no picker: `hall-of-fame/{userId}` already holds the rankings, and `summarizeHallOfFame(data, today)` only turns the stored slices into boards. "Today" is a PARAMETER (`HallOfFameToday`), never `new Date()` inside the module — a test pins a month instead of racing the calendar.
- **`hallOfFameRecords.ts` is the ONE definition of a record AND of a ranking.** `buildHallOfFameRankings` is called by BOTH writers (`hallOfFameService.ts` client, `hallOfFameService.server.ts` Admin) and the periodic email reads the same builders. The client used to carry its own copy of `calculateMonthlyRecords`/`calculateYearlyRecords`; it was collapsed on 2026-08-25. Never re-implement a ranking in a page, a route or a component.
- **How a stale document heals, and when it does not** (verified 2026-08-25): the nightly cron calls `updateHallOfFame` **only inside `if (snapshotResult.success)`** (`app/api/cron/monthly-snapshot/route.ts`), and `POST /api/portfolio/snapshot` answers `success: false` with «No assets found for user» — so an account with **no assets never heals from the cron**, only from the page's «Aggiorna i record», and in **demo** that button is disabled by `useDemoMode()`. Never document a new stored field as "the cron will fill it in".
- **One document per OWNER, not per viewer**: the page reads `hall-of-fame/{ownerId}` (`useActiveAccount()`, never `user.uid`), so a co-owner of a shared account reads the SAME document and the owner's recalculation covers them both. Only that person's own separate account is a second document.
- **`updateHallOfFame` reads the notes back before its full `set`**: a ranking update must never cost the user a note. Both writers do it; anything that touches that path keeps the read-back.
- **Two rankings and a stats block are OPTIONAL on the document** (`bestMonthsBySavings`, `bestYearsBySavings`, `stats`): they arrived on 2026-08-25, so a document written before that has none. `getBoard` returns **null** for a ranking the document does not carry — distinct from an empty board, which means "nothing ever qualified" — and the tile says the record arrives with the next update instead of printing a zero (The Narrative Honesty Rule). Do not "fix" it with `?? []`.
- **A savings record needs income** (`rankBySavings`): without the `totalIncome > 0` guard the winner is systematically the month with the least DATA — an untracked month saves as much as one that earned nothing and outranks every real month. The tile's footer states the guard. Known blind spot: a month with income and zero expenses recorded reads as a 100% rate.
- **`stats` cannot be recovered from the rankings** — the document keeps only the top slices, so the average monthly income that «il 62,1% sopra la tua media mensile» divides by is stored, not derived. Same for the month/year counts the compact header prints.
- **The verdict names the BEST month, the tile's footer the WORST** — never the same figure twice (the rule Storico settled). When the running month IS the record the headline says so (`Agosto 2026 è il tuo mese migliore.`) and the sentence drops its own «al 3° posto» clause. A podium place gets a word (`il secondo anno migliore`), a place past the third gets its number (`al 4° posto tra gli anni`): «il settimo anno migliore» reads as praise it is not.
- **The chart dates, the podium ranks** (`buildRecordTimeline`): the twelve record months are drawn in CHRONOLOGICAL order, because «when did the records happen?» is a different question from «which are they» and the One-Tile-One-Question Rule forbids the same rows twice. Beyond the limit the SMALLEST records are dropped, never the oldest — cutting by date would silently make it a recent-months chart.
- **A cost is carried POSITIVE and uncoloured** (`valueOf`, `readingOf` in `RecordRows`): an expense record is the size of a cost, not a loss, and the sign tokens mean gain and loss and nothing else. A savings rate is a proportion, so it is unsigned beside a signed amount. The bar takes the slot of the QUANTITY ranked — `--chart-2` for income and savings, `--chart-1` for net worth and spending.
- **The `Mensile|Annuale` + category switcher lives in the «Dettaglio» disclosure**, not above the grid: on the grid each ranking is already a tile, so a switcher over them would answer the same question twice. Below `desktop:` the two pills stack, take 44px targets and each scrolls inside its own strip (`-mx-5 px-5`) — side by side they are 464px of controls in a 318px tile.
- **`NoteTrigger` hides itself when the period has no note**, so a ranking without notes grows no ghost column; inside the Dettaglio's table it takes `alwaysVisible`, because a «Nota» column whose marker only a mouse can reveal promises an empty column.
- **Three fields are stored WITH the rankings and never derived on the page** (2026-09-24): `rankingsUpdatedAt` (when the rankings were rebuilt — `updatedAt` moves on every note save, so it cannot date them; the header prints it), `stats.sinceWorstMonth` (the months after the worst one and how many grew — the Record footer's «da allora 32 mesi su 35 in crescita»; not recoverable from the top-20 slices) and `YearlyRecord.monthsCovered` (a first year that starts in December is ranked beside whole years and says «2 mesi»; `isPartialYear` decides, the running year says «finora» instead). All three are optional: a document written before them drops the clause (The Narrative Honesty Rule). Both writers stamp them in ONE place, `updateHallOfFame`; a note writer spreads the existing document and must keep doing so.
- **The tile under the verdict never repeats the verdict's figure** (2026-09-24): the verdict names the record month with its two figures, the Record reading says what the podium is worth and where the tile ends (`describeNetWorthRecords` takes `last`), the Anni reading the DISTANCE from the place above (`rowAboveCurrent`, unsigned and uncoloured: a gap is neither a gain nor a loss) where the verdict already gave the rank. `describeRecordWindow` is the Record aside («da dic 2022 a set 2026»): the month count is the header's.
- **An article before a figure comes from `patrimonioNarrative`'s helpers** (`articleForPercent`, `pluralArticleFor`), never typed: «il 88,5%» and «Gli 10 anni» shipped for a month. A ranking is ordered in its own terms (`RANKING_ORDER`): a cost is «dal più alto», a decline «dal più forte», only growth and savings «dal migliore».
- **A note is filed FROM the row** (2026-09-24): `NoteTrigger` renders «Aggiungi una nota a marzo 2024» on a row without a note — on the tiles only under a mouse (hover/focus, `[@media(pointer:fine)]`), in the Dettaglio's «Nota» column always — and hands the page a `NotePrefill` (year, month, section) that `HallOfFameNoteDialog` seeds on open; the form's reading says when the chosen period sits in none of the chosen rankings (`isPeriodRanked`). Both windows take `returnFocusTo` from the opener (`noteOpenerRef`); a second note on the same period and ranking is still reachable only from the Note tile (`matching[0]`).
- **The rankings have ONE vocabulary, the tiles'**: `SECTION_LABELS` («Crescita del patrimonio · mese») names a ranking wherever a note mentions it, `SECTION_SUBJECTS` the same without the period for a list grouped under «Mensili» / «Annuali» (the form's two `fieldset`s, the view's lists). Adding a ranking means adding its `HallOfFameSectionKey`, BOTH label entries AND its place in `MONTHLY_SECTION_KEYS`/`YEARLY_SECTION_KEYS`.
- **The record axis prints the year once per year** (`TimelinePoint.yearLabel`, under the first bar of each year, a second grid row outside the SVG); the caption says «I 12 record più grandi» when the chart drops some of the twenty (`describeTimelineCaption`).
- **Playwright locators**: the verdict is `heading level 2` and its accessible name has NO full stop (the tone dot is its own span) — below `desktop:` the level-2 headings are TWO, the verdict and «Il resto della pagina», so the verdict is found by name or `.first()`; a tile is `region` named by its `ariaLabel` («Record del patrimonio», «Gli anni con la crescita di patrimonio più alta», «Classifica completa»), and below `desktop:` a closed row holds NOTHING — a spec opens it through `#<id>-trigger` before reading inside (the Dettaglio keeps its own `aria-expanded` button, not a `-trigger` id); the strip is `list` «Le cifre del verdetto», its cells `button`s named «{label} {value}, apre {eyebrow}»; «Il perché · …» is a `button` with `aria-expanded` and no id; the Dettaglio's pills are `radio`s in a `radiogroup`; the note windows are `dialog`s named by their title («Aggiungi una nota», «La tua nota»); the markers are «Leggi la nota di marzo 2024» / «Aggiungi una nota a marzo 2024». The full table's scroller carries a `mask-image` only while `scrollWidth > clientWidth`, measured.

## Composizione mobile

The sample page of the small-screen composition (2026-10-10, PR #450; its spec, retired the same day, is in `git log -- doc/mobile/`): below `desktop:` the page is composed, at 1440 it is the page above with one change of punctuation. The rules are proposed for DESIGN.md by MOB-09; here is what this page does with them.

- **THE tile**: Record del patrimonio, first in the DOM and open at every width, never a row (decision 21); below `desktop:` its chart yields — `RecordBars` at `min-h-[120px]` (`desktop:min-h-[130px]`), the podium keeps its five rows, the reading and the footer stay (decision 4). On the fixture `census@example.com` the first closed row still ends under the pill at 390 and at 1024 (above it at 768): `firstClosedRowAbovePill` is registered `false` with `raisedBy: "MOB-02: …"` (decisions 19 and 25, `doc/mobile/README.md` § 3.1).
- **The strip** (`selectHallOfFameStrip(summary)`, `lib/utils/hallOfFameSummary.ts`; a cell draws the unit as its own span after a `ml-[0.2em]`, because in Geist Mono the no-break space before «€» is a whole cell and at 22px it read as a gap — the tour of 2026-10-10): «Quest'anno» → `annual:growth` `current.value` → opens `hof-anni`; «Entrate record» → `monthly:income` `top.value` → `hof-entrate`; «Risparmio record» → `monthly:savings` `top.value` → `hof-risparmio`; each cell names the row it opens from the controller's `sections.eyebrows` («, apre Anni»; 2026-10-10, the retirement: the strip does not know the rows, so a missing id fell back to the cell's label). Each prints EXACTLY what the owning sentence prints (`__tests__/hallOfFameSummary.test.ts` holds the identity). «Quest'anno» is the verdict's own figure and may show again in the opened «Il perché» (decision 8).
- **Lifted blocks**: none. The three cells repeat the rows' readings and the first line of their rankings, which stay whole (decision 15); the first page that lifts is the Panoramica (MOB-03).
- **The rows and their aside, in DOM order** (`HOF_SECTIONS` in the page, ids from `HOF_SECTION_IDS`): `hof-entrate` («per mese»), `hof-risparmio` («entrate − spese»), `hof-anni` («crescita del patrimonio»), `hof-note` (`describeClosedNotesAside`: «nessuna nota» / «una nota» / «4 note»); a text aside keeps its place beside the eyebrow when the row is open (the three boards), a control aside (Note's «Aggiungi») opens the panel's first row instead; then the Dettaglio as it is, then the two actions «Aggiungi una nota» and «Aggiorna i record» (decision 25; in the empty state they stay under the verdict, there is no Dettaglio). All four closed on a first visit (`defaultOpen` empty); `HOF_SECTIONS` is module-level, as every page's `sections` and `defaultOpen` must be (a literal array rebuilds every handler on every render). «Chiudi tutte» keeps the content of rows open at load, closed and inert (seen red in the retirement, 2026-10-10). `TILE_ROW_CELL_CLASS` on their cells: at 768 a closed row beside an open one would otherwise stretch to its height.
- **`binding` clauses and the `scope`**: none (a page that passes a `scope` gets it rendered twice, after the strip below `desktop:` and under the paragraph at 1440 — one sequence cannot sit in both places; the caption carries no id and no focus). The verdict is two sentences (`buildHallOfFameVerdict`): the record's figures close the first («…il +4,1% in un mese.»), the running year and month make the second («Il 2026 è finora…, e settembre è oggi al 4° posto tra i mesi.»; without a year «Settembre è oggi…»), `leadLength` on the boundary, `restLabel` «l'anno e il mese in corso» (or one of the two); with neither there is no second sentence and no cut. The «; il 2026» of before became «. Il 2026» at 1440 too (decision 5).
- **Absences**: as before. Loading, nothing recorded and a failed read are the whole page's (one payload): no row, no strip, no `PageRest`, no announcement; the strip's `null` cells say why («in calo quest'anno», «non ancora misurato», «nessuna entrata registrata», «nessun mese con entrate», «arriva con il prossimo aggiornamento»).
- **The memory**: `mobile-sections:hall-of-fame` (per device and browser, not per account; absent or unreadable → the page's defaults, everything closed). «Il perché» is remembered as the row `perche`; «Apri tutte»/«Chiudi tutte» never touch it.
- **768/1024 and the specs**: the same composition at 768 (two columns: Record full, Entrate beside Risparmio, Anni and Note full) and at 1024, until MOB-08 decides the tablet's columns. `e2e/mobile-composition.hof.mobile.spec.ts` (nine tests at 390, the first screen measured against the pill's top, the clock frozen at 15 settembre 2026 so the fixture's running month and year hold whatever the day), `e2e/hall-of-fame.hof.mobile.spec.ts` (opens Anni before reading it), `e2e/hall-of-fame.hof.spec.ts` (1440: no trigger, no strip, no «Il perché», one `<p>`).

## Per-page blind spots

- **Hall of Fame**: the two savings rankings and `stats` do not exist in documents written before 2026-08-25, and `rankingsUpdatedAt`, `stats.sinceWorstMonth` and `monthsCovered` before 2026-09-24 — until a recalculation the Risparmio tile explains instead of ranking, the header carries no date, the Record footer no recovery, a partial year no chip; the nightly cron recalculates only AFTER a successful snapshot (an account without assets has only «Aggiorna i record», disabled in demo). A month with income and ZERO expenses reads 100% savings; Note sorts by annotated PERIOD; the 12-record chart drops the SMALLEST records past the twelfth; a note survives its period leaving the top twenty; the «+» to add a note from a tile row is a MOUSE affordance (on touch the Dettaglio's «Nota» column has it); below 769px no modal takes the focus on open (doc/guide/dialog.md). The E2E fixture's «running month» is settembre 2026: from October the `hof` specs that name «set 2026 · ORA» read the fixture as a closed history and stay green, the verdict's running-month clause simply drops — except `e2e/mobile-composition.hof.mobile.spec.ts`, which freezes the browser's clock at 15 settembre 2026 before every load and reads the fixture as it was written. Below `desktop:` a never-opened row's panel is EMPTY (the content mounts on the first opening): a locator that finds nothing inside Entrate, Risparmio, Anni or Note is reading a closed row, not a missing tile. The strip's «Quest'anno» is the verdict's figure printed twice on purpose (decision 8).
